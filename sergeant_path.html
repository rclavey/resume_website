Practice in different ways

Use flashcards, quizzes, matching, and recall exercises with typo-tolerant answers. Practice specific legal and supervisory situations, compare plausible alternatives, and study juvenile law in its own section.

Return to what needs work

FSRS-6 spaced review uses your recall history and time since review, with published default parameters and a 90% retention target. Set a daily time goal, track study sessions and streaks, and see your progress across nine study areas in a radar chart.

Keep a source-backed plan

Read explanations with source links, keep personal notes, and track assigned reading in your own books. Bring in updated study packs locally when your resources change.

Know what your progress means

The initial plan uses the verified 2026 Massachusetts Civil Service Police Sergeant reading list as a baseline. That list assigns portions of the 2025 Massachusetts Police Manual, specified 2026 Supplement sections, selected chapters of Supervision of Police Personnel, and all of Workplace Gold. The commercial books are not included in the app.

The countdown starts with a provisional study target of September 15, 2027. It is not an official examination date. Reconcile the plan with the official 2027 posting, reading list and candidate instructions when available.

Practice questions and judgment explanations are independently authored. They are not official examination questions or scoring keys. Difficulty labels and practice results are study tools, not a prediction of an official score. Baseline completion requires your assigned-reading attestations, all included lessons, delayed recall across the question bank, and no overdue reviews. It records study progress, not independent verification of your books or a guarantee of passing.

Sergeant Path is independently developed by Richard Lavey. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Massachusetts HRD, MPTC, POST, or any police department. Consult HRD's official reading lists and guides for controlling examination requirements.

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

This policy explains how Sergeant Path, developed by Richard Lavey, handles information in the iPhone and iPad app.

1. Information stored on your device

Sergeant Path does not require an account. Your study goals, target date, quiz responses, review schedule, study-time records, streaks, reading checkmarks, personal notes, settings and imported study packs are stored locally on your device. This information is used on the device to provide study activities, review recommendations and progress statistics.

2. No automatic developer collection

The app does not send your study data to the developer and has no developer-operated backend. It does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics. The developer does not sell or rent app data.

3. Optional reminders

If you choose to enable reminders, the app requests notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device. A server is not used to send these study reminders. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

4. Backups, imports and sharing

You may choose to export a progress backup or study pack, restore a backup, or import a study pack using the iOS file interface. Processing takes place locally. A progress backup can contain your study history and personal notes. You control where an exported file is saved or shared and should review it before sending it to another person or service.

Before a progress restore or library change, the app may retain a local copy of your previous progress to help protect against accidental loss. A local library snapshot helps keep progress for unchanged content when the app is updated. Files you explicitly share with other services are handled under those services' policies. iOS may include app files in a device backup according to your Apple settings; Sergeant Path does not provide its own cloud synchronization.

5. External resources

Source, privacy and support links open external pages or your email application when you choose them. The destination may receive ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, and may have its own privacy practices. The app does not attach your study history or notes to source links.

6. Retention and deletion

Local study information remains until you reset it or remove the app. The in-app progress reset removes app-managed progress, personal notes and retained progress copies. Deleting the app removes its local application data, subject to normal iOS behavior. Files that you exported elsewhere and device backups remain under your control and may need to be deleted separately through the relevant service or device settings.

7. Support correspondence

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you choose to include. This correspondence is used to answer your request and resolve app issues, and is retained as needed for that purpose. Please do not send operational records, personal information about other people, or confidential material. No diagnostic upload occurs automatically.

8. Children's privacy

Sergeant Path is intended for adult promotional-exam candidates. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Policy changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised date. For privacy questions or a request concerning support correspondence, email updates@realexpense.com.

Support

For help or feedback, email updates@realexpense.com. Include the app version, iOS version and a short description of the issue. Progress exports are optional and are not needed for an initial support request.

Report a content issue

Include the question or lesson identifier, the source citation, and the rule or explanation you believe needs review. Do not include real case details or confidential documents. Use the official exam-year source and current primary law to check a disputed point.

Moving to another device

Use the progress export in Settings to save a backup, then restore it on the new device. Keep a copy somewhere you control. Restoring replaces the current progress after confirmation.

Examination questions

For official eligibility, application, accommodations, scoring or examination instructions, use Massachusetts Civil Service. App support cannot make official examination decisions.
