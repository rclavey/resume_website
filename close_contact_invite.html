Checking this invite

Close Contact is checking the private token in this link.

Paste the token into the optional referral field during Close Contact account setup. Keep it private. The server checks whether it is valid, unused, and eligible.

What the invite can do

A valid invite can assign the role saved by the sender. It cannot change the game rules, reveal nearby players, or award referral points by itself. Referral rewards require the server's activity and fraud checks.
