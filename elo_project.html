Model notes

Methodology & Calibration

Elo ratings

Ratings update after each recorded professional MMA fight. A fighter keeps one identity and rating while moving between promotions, making cross-promotion comparisons possible.

Matchup projections

The Matchup Lab supports fighters from different organizations. It defaults to the detailed-stat ensemble when both fighters are eligible and automatically falls back to the shared Elo rating when required telemetry is missing.

Fair implied odds

Fair American and decimal odds are calculated directly from each projected probability with no sportsbook margin. The displayed 95% ranges visualize model uncertainty and are not guarantees.

Fight-card model

Upcoming cards from every collected promotion are shown together. Each matchup uses the original detailed-stat ensemble when eligible and an Elo-only prediction otherwise.

Insufficient data

Unknown fighters start from a calibrated promotion prior. Fighters without the required detailed statistics are excluded from component and ensemble models instead of borrowing or manufacturing telemetry.

Free data sources

Results and ratings combine public ESPN and MIT-licensed Kaggle records. Detailed telemetry uses UFCStats plus attributable UFCalendar bout pages when complete; partial statistics remain visible to the data pipeline but never enter model training.
