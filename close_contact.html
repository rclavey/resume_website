Close Contact

Close Contact turns the world around you into a cartoon zombie survival game for iPhone. Build both forms of your character, equip gear from the Armory, launch nearby Hunt Sessions, and fight for survival as each month brings a new outbreak.

Survive a world that changes every month

Each numbered Game begins with fresh roles, health, armor, and monthly rankings. Survivors build Valor by exploring, staying active, taking down the Infected, and bringing new players into the fight. Infected players build Dread through damage, conversions, travel, and referrals. A confirmed takedown can turn a fallen Survivor into part of the horde, shifting the balance of the outbreak in real time. Monthly boards keep every season competitive, while lifetime rankings record your full survival story.

Players never pick a side or switch whenever they want. Close Contact assigns every role automatically. During a Game, a Survivor becomes Infected only if server-confirmed combat brings their health to zero. Once infected, they stay Infected for the rest of that Game. If they survive until the Game ends, they never change sides in that Game. Your Survivor streak counts the time you stay alive as a Survivor, even across monthly Games. If a new Game assigns you as Infected, the streak pauses without ending or growing. It picks up where it left off if a later Game assigns you as a Survivor. Only being defeated and infected resets the current streak. Your longest streak stays saved.

Nearby Hunt Sessions

Start a Hunt Session when you are ready to play. Close Contact uses nearby signals to turn other participating iPhones into anonymous encounters, with broad range and danger bands that build suspense without revealing who another player is.

Two forms, one character

Design distinct Survivor and Infected looks with character choices, skin tones, hairstyles, eye colors, idle animations, clothing, and field gear. Body-fitted cartoon artwork makes clothing look worn and keeps field equipment anchored to the character. Each form remembers its own wardrobe and equipment.

Build your survival loadout

The Armory includes 35 illustrated items across clothing, armor, weapons, and field gear. Earn Scrap through play or buy an optional consumable Scrap pack through Apple. Gear has fixed prices, wears down through use, and can be repaired with Scrap before it breaks. Weapons, sensors, and other field gear have limited uses.

Factions, seasons, and rivalries

Create a faction, recruit allies, compare shared stats, and return for a new outbreak every month. Scrap, permanent unlocks, faction membership, and lifetime scores carry your progress forward while each monthly Game creates a fresh race for the top.

Nearby play with privacy built in

Close Contact creates the tension of a nearby zombie encounter without showing another player's identity, direction, route, or exact distance. Phones exchange short-lived game credentials and display only broad count and danger bands. Encounter results are verified by the game service, and limited phone-to-phone distance samples are used only to confirm play, not to create a location history.

Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Close Contact processes, why it is needed, and the choices available to you. Close Contact is an iPhone zombie survival game developed by Richard Lavey for the United States App Store. This policy describes the release app and service.

1. Account and Profile Information

Close Contact uses Sign in with Apple and Firebase Authentication. Apple provides an account identifier and may provide your name or Apple email address, which the app passes to Firebase Authentication when supplied. You may use Hide My Email, and Close Contact never receives your Apple Account password.

The service stores information needed to run your account and the game. This includes your callsign, opaque public player identifier, assigned role, avatar appearance, role-specific clothing and equipment, item durability and remaining uses, level and XP, health, armor, inventory, Scrap balance and debt, monthly and lifetime scores, current and longest Survivor streak state, ranks, faction membership, referrals, broad outbreak cell, account status, and Game history.

2. Nearby Interaction and Bluetooth

During a player-started Hunt Session, Close Contact uses Bluetooth Low Energy to discover other participating devices and exchange rotating anonymous encounter credentials. Supported iPhones may also exchange Apple Nearby Interaction discovery tokens. Discovery tokens, nearby Bluetooth identifiers, and direction vectors remain on the phones and are not sent to the game service.

Nearby devices do not receive your callsign, Apple identifier, email address, route, or geographic location. When two players submit matching evidence, the service may receive expiring encounter proofs, a limited set of one-second phone-to-phone distance samples, and combat results. These samples measure the distance between participating devices. They are not latitude or longitude and do not create a geographic route.

3. Motion, Location, and Travel

If you allow location access, Close Contact uses one recent location sample during account setup and again before a Hunt Session. Your iPhone converts the sample into a broad half-degree outbreak cell before information leaves the device. The game service receives the cell identifier, not the original location sample.

During a Hunt Session, the app uses motion activity, pedometer distance, and location-based speed on the iPhone to identify vehicle or unsafe speeds, reject implausible movement, and calculate eligible walking or running. The service may receive the broad outbreak cell, eligible distance, active duration, automotive duration, and a coarse daily distance band. It does not receive or store your geographic coordinates, route, raw location samples, or raw motion signals. Other players cannot see your outbreak cell.

4. Gameplay, Referrals, Factions, and Leaderboards

The service receives brief Hunt Session check-ins and limited mutual encounter evidence. It controls lasting game state, rewards, Armory purchases, combat results, monthly resets, and rankings so that nearby devices cannot rewrite those results.

Referral invites use expiring tokens, and faction invites use private codes. A valid referral can set a new player's starting role only before that player begins normal play. Later monthly roles are assigned automatically. Invite activity and rewards must pass the game's activity and fraud checks.

Public leaderboard results may show a callsign, role-side score, rank, and finalized placement. Faction members can compare shared scores and can see an assigned role only when that player enables role sharing. Faction rosters do not show exact health, live presence, encounter details, routes, devices, locations, or directions.

5. Live Activities and Notifications

During a Hunt Session, a Live Activity may show your role, health, safety status, Game number, and broad Survivor and Infected count bands on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island. It does not show a nearby callsign, exact distance, direction, or geographic location. A Hunt Session and its Live Activity end after no more than eight hours.

Version 1 does not request notification permission or store a device push-notification token. Apple App Store Server Notifications are server-to-server purchase and refund updates, not player push notifications.

6. Purchases and Advertising

Close Contact offers six optional consumable Scrap packs through Apple's In-App Purchase system: 100, 550, 1,150, 2,400, 6,250, or 13,000 Scrap. Players can also earn Scrap through eligible Hunt time and monthly results without paying. Scrap buys fixed gear, repairs, replacement instances for eligible unlocked items, Field Medkits, and fixed XP offers. Buying Scrap cannot choose a side, build a Survivor streak, award Valor or Dread, decide combat, qualify a referral, change a leaderboard result, or bypass a Hunt or safety rule. There are no loot boxes or randomized purchase outcomes.

Apple handles the customer's payment method. Close Contact does not receive or store card or bank details, so it does not collect Payment Info. Close Contact does collect linked Purchase History for App Functionality, including product and transaction identifiers, signed transaction verification, account binding, Scrap delivery, refund state, debt, and retry records. This lets the service credit each verified purchase once, recover an interrupted delivery, and reconcile refunds or refund reversals.

Close Contact has no subscriptions, third-party advertising, advertising identifier, cross-app tracking, sale of personal information, or gambling.

7. Service Providers and Diagnostics

Close Contact uses Apple for Sign in with Apple, Live Activities, App Store distribution, StoreKit payment processing, and App Store Server Notifications. It uses Google Firebase and Google Cloud for authentication, app-integrity checks, server functions in us-central1, and game storage in the United States multi-region location nam5. This public website is hosted by GitHub Pages.

Firebase Authentication may collect unlinked technical diagnostic data for Analytics and a linked User ID for App Functionality. Close Contact does not include the Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, advertising, or attribution products. Firebase and Google Cloud may also process account identifiers, app-integrity evidence, operational logs, and technical information needed to provide and protect the service. Learn more in Firebase's privacy and security documentation.

8. Retention, Export, and Account Deletion

The following periods describe the release service. Expiry makes a record eligible for asynchronous deletion, so removal may not happen at the exact expiry time.

  • Rotating encounter credentials use five-minute slots and remain valid for no more than about ten minutes.
  • Matched encounter claims and rounded phone-to-phone distance evidence expire after 24 hours.
  • Operation retry receipts expire after 30 days.
  • Hunt presence sessions use a renewable 90-second lease. Ended, expired, or abandoned sessions become eligible for deletion after the lease expires.
  • Referral invites expire within seven days and may expire sooner near the end of a Game.
  • Movement evidence, Game history, lifetime scores, Survivor streak state, inventory, player-facing Purchase History, the currency ledger, Scrap balance, and Scrap debt remain while the account is active and are included in the account deletion process.
  • Moderation reports and audit records may remain after deletion for safety and enforcement. The service removes account and public-player identifiers, removes appeal statements, and marks retained records as belonging to a deleted player. These minimized records do not currently have an age-based expiry.
  • Limited Apple transaction, server-notification, one-way account-token binding, callsign-reservation, and deletion-completion records may remain for replay prevention, refund handling, impersonation prevention, and deletion retry safety. Close Contact removes the account ID and public-profile link where implemented, but these limited records do not currently have an age-based expiry.

The Google Cloud default log bucket retains entries for 30 days. Google locks required administrative and system audit logs for 400 days. Firestore point-in-time recovery is disabled, and the configured staging and production projects do not have scheduled Firestore backups.

The app keeps a protected local snapshot so it can resume after relaunch. Signing out clears the cached release snapshot from the phone. A cloud export includes the player-facing account sections and leaves out raw peer identifiers, routes, security tokens, encounter evidence, and free-form report text. Because the export is created while the account is active, records can change during the process and records already removed under retention rules will not appear. Use Outbreak > Profile & Settings > Account > Create cloud account export.

Use Outbreak > Profile & Settings > Account > Delete cloud account to request deletion. The service removes or anonymizes the Firebase Authentication account, public profile, Game participation, leaderboards, factions, Hunt Session, encounter and movement records, referrals and outstanding invites, inventory, player-facing Purchase History, and currency ledger. A normalized callsign may remain reserved without the former account ID to prevent impersonation. The minimized safety, purchase, replay-prevention, and deletion records described above may remain. Cloud logs follow the provider retention periods above.

9. Choices, Security, and Integrity

You can stop a Hunt Session at any time, deny or revoke permissions in iOS Settings, and control optional faction role sharing. Close Contact uses authenticated connections, Firebase App Check with Apple App Attest, restricted database rules, rotating encounter credentials, and server-calculated game state. These safeguards reduce risk, but no service can guarantee absolute security.

For help with access, correction, export, or deletion, contact updates@realexpense.com. Send the request from the email path associated with the account when possible and include your public callsign or player ID if known. Close Contact may require a fresh sign-in or another limited ownership check. Never email a password, Apple credential, Firebase token, App Check token, or recovery code.

10. Children's Privacy

Close Contact is not directed to children under 13. The app does not currently use independent age assurance. The game includes cartoon horror and simulated fantasy combat.

Someone who travels or previously downloaded the app may still be able to use or update it. Version 1 does not add a separate runtime location or account-region gate. Safety Rules

You are responsible for your choices. Play only within the law and safe limits. Close Contact is a game, not permission to touch, chase, follow, surround, threaten, restrain, or block another person. Stay aware, obey all laws and property rules, keep your phone down while moving, and stop immediately when anyone asks for space. Never play while driving, cycling, operating equipment, or moving at an unsafe speed.

The game pauses combat inside the two-meter buffer and pauses rewards when movement appears unsafe. Radio, motion, and location signals can be delayed, blocked, or wrong, so treat every distance or count as approximate. Disconnecting or closing the app never causes an automatic infection or opponent reward and never justifies physical pursuit.

Community Guidelines

Do not use a callsign or faction name to harass, threaten, impersonate, spam, share someone's personal information, promote hate, post sexual or exploitative material, or encourage illegal or dangerous activity. Play only with willing participants. If anyone is in immediate danger, leave the area and contact local emergency services.
