Close Contact

Close Contact turns the world around you into a cartoon zombie survival game for iPhone. Build both forms of your character, equip gear from the Armory, launch nearby Hunt Sessions, and fight for survival as each month brings a new outbreak.

Survive a world that changes every month

Each numbered Game begins with fresh roles, health, armor, and monthly rankings. Survivors build Valor by exploring, staying active, taking down the Infected, and bringing new players into the fight. Infected players build Dread through damage, conversions, travel, and referrals. A confirmed takedown can turn a fallen Survivor into part of the horde, shifting the balance of the outbreak in real time. Monthly boards keep every season competitive, while lifetime rankings record your full survival story.

Players never pick a side or switch whenever they want. Close Contact assigns every role automatically. During a Game, a Survivor becomes Infected only if server-confirmed combat brings their health to zero. Once infected, they stay Infected for the rest of that Game. If they survive until the Game ends, they never change sides in that Game. Your Survivor streak counts the time you stay alive as a Survivor, even across monthly Games. If a new Game assigns you as Infected, the streak pauses without ending or growing. It picks up where it left off if a later Game assigns you as a Survivor. Only being defeated and infected resets the current streak. Your longest streak stays saved.

Nearby Hunt Sessions

Start a Hunt Session when you are ready to play. Close Contact uses nearby signals to turn other participating iPhones into anonymous encounters, with broad range and danger bands that build suspense without revealing who another player is.

Two forms, one character

Design distinct Survivor and Infected looks with character choices, skin tones, hairstyles, eye colors, idle animations, clothing, and field gear. Body-fitted cartoon artwork makes clothing look worn and keeps field equipment anchored to the character. Each form remembers its own wardrobe and equipment.

Build your survival loadout

The Armory includes 35 illustrated items across clothing, armor, weapons, and field gear. Earn Scrap through play, unlock equipment at clear fixed prices, and combine protection with specialized tools that can improve your awareness during a Hunt.

Factions, seasons, and rivalries

Create a faction, recruit allies, compare shared stats, and return for a new outbreak every month. Scrap, permanent gear, faction membership, and lifetime scores carry your progress forward while each monthly Game creates a fresh race for the top.

Nearby play with privacy built in

Close Contact creates the tension of a nearby zombie encounter without showing another player's identity, direction, route, or exact distance. Phones exchange short-lived game tickets and display only broad count and danger bands. Encounter results are verified by the game service, and rounded phone-to-phone range samples are used only to confirm play, not to create a location history.

Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Close Contact processes, why it is needed, and the choices available to you. Close Contact is an iPhone zombie survival game developed by Richard Lavey and planned for release in the United States. The app is currently in testing. Tester profiles remain on the device, and the production account service is not yet live. The sections below describe both the tester build and the planned release service.

1. Account and Profile Information

The tester build uses a local profile. The planned release version will use Sign in with Apple and Firebase Authentication. Apple provides an account identifier and, only if you choose to share them, your name and email address. You may use Hide My Email, and Close Contact never receives your Apple Account password.

The release service will store the information needed to run your account and the game. This includes your callsign, game role, avatar design, clothing and equipment, game identifiers, scores, health, Survivor streak, Scrap, inventory, referrals, faction membership, broad outbreak region, and Game history. Safety acknowledgements and ordinary preferences remain on your iPhone.

2. Nearby Interaction and Bluetooth

During a Hunt Session, Close Contact uses Bluetooth Low Energy to discover other participating devices and exchange rotating anonymous encounter tickets. Supported iPhones may also exchange Apple Nearby Interaction discovery tokens. Discovery tokens and the continuous ranging stream remain on the phones. Each phone calculates distance locally, shows only a broad band, and clears session ranging data when the Hunt ends.

Nearby devices do not receive your callsign, Apple identifier, email address, route, or precise location. The release service may receive expiring encounter proofs, short timestamps, and a few rounded measurements of the distance between participating phones. This limited information confirms mutual play and prevents duplicate rewards. It is not geographic location data and is not used to build a route.

3. Motion, Location, and Travel

If you allow location access, Close Contact uses one location sample during account setup and again before a Hunt Session. Your iPhone converts the sample into a broad half-degree outbreak cell before information leaves the device. The game service receives the cell identifier, not the original location sample.

During a visible Hunt Session, the app uses motion activity, step distance, and location-based speed to identify vehicle or unsafe speeds, reject implausible movement, and calculate eligible walking or running. The service may receive the broad outbreak cell and an eligible pedestrian-distance total. It does not receive or store your precise coordinates, route, raw location samples, or raw motion samples. Other players cannot see your outbreak cell.

4. Gameplay, Referrals, Factions, and Leaderboards

The release service will receive brief session check-ins and limited mutual encounter evidence. The service controls lasting game state, rewards, Armory purchases, combat results, monthly resets, and rankings so that nearby devices cannot rewrite those results.

Referral invites use expiring tokens, and faction invites use private codes. Invite activity and rewards must pass the game's activity and fraud checks.

Public leaderboards show a callsign, score, rank when available, and finalized end-of-Game result. A current role or faction is not included. Faction members can compare shared scores and can see a role only when that member enables role sharing. Faction rosters do not include exact health, live presence, encounter details, routes, devices, location, or direction.

5. Live Activities and Notifications

During a Hunt Session, a Live Activity may show your role, health, safety status, Game number, and broad Survivor and Infected count bands on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island. It does not show nearby callsigns, exact distance, direction, or precise location. A Hunt Session and its Live Activity end after no more than eight hours.

The tester build does not request notification permission. If notifications are included in the release and you allow them, the service may store a Firebase Cloud Messaging token to deliver game updates. Notification access can be changed in iOS Settings.

6. Purchases and Advertising

The planned initial release uses earned in-game Scrap. It does not include loot boxes, randomized paid rewards, third-party advertising, an advertising identifier, cross-app tracking, or the sale of personal information. This policy and the App Store disclosures will be updated before any future real-money purchase feature becomes available.

7. Service Providers and Processing

The planned release service will use Google Firebase for authentication, cloud storage, server logic, abuse prevention, and service reliability. Firebase may process account identifiers, technical device information, operational logs, and diagnostics needed to provide and protect the game. It may also deliver notifications if that feature is included. Learn more in Firebase's privacy and security documentation.

8. Retention and Account Deletion

The app keeps a protected local game snapshot so it can resume after relaunch. Signing out clears the cached release snapshot from the phone. The tester build does not create a production cloud account, so its local export contains only information saved on that iPhone.

The release service is designed to retain game data while your account is active and as needed for seasons, leaderboards, Scrap records, security, and legal obligations. Its account export will include player-facing account and game information while excluding security tokens, raw peer identifiers, encounter evidence, and report text.

A completed account deletion request is designed to remove the authentication account, private profile, inventory, faction membership, and other account-linked operational data. A one-way record may remain to confirm that deletion finished without retaining the former account identifier. Production backup retention and deletion timing will be finalized and added to this policy before release.

9. Security and Integrity

The release service is designed to use authenticated connections, Firebase App Check with Apple App Attest, restricted database rules, rotating encounter tickets, and server-calculated game state. These safeguards reduce risk, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Use the contact below to report a suspected account compromise or privacy issue.

10. Children's Privacy

Close Contact is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. The game includes mild cartoon horror and simulated combat. If you believe a child has provided information, contact the developer for review and deletion.

11. United States Availability and International Processing

The app is planned for the United States App Store only. Richard Lavey and service providers may still process information in the United States and other countries where they operate, which may have different data-protection laws.

12. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised, and additional in-app notice will be provided when required.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, account-deletion assistance, or a safety concern, email updates@realexpense.com.

Safety Rules

You are responsible for your choices. Play only within the law and safe limits. Close Contact is a game, not permission to touch, chase, follow, surround, threaten, restrain, or block another person. Stay aware, obey all laws and property rules, keep your phone down while moving, and stop immediately when anyone asks for space. Never play while driving or at an unsafe speed.

The game is designed to pause scoring inside the two-meter buffer and when movement appears unsafe. Radio and motion signals can be delayed, blocked, or unavailable, so treat every distance or count as approximate. Disconnecting or closing the app never justifies physical pursuit and does not create an automatic penalty.

Community Guidelines

Do not use a callsign or faction name to harass, threaten, impersonate, spam, share someone's personal information, promote hate, post sexual or exploitative material, or encourage illegal or dangerous activity. During testing, play only with people you know and trust. If anyone is in immediate danger, leave the game and contact local emergency services.

Support

For help, feedback, account-deletion assistance, or a safety concern, email updates@realexpense.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not email passwords, precise locations, encounter identities, or other sensitive information.
