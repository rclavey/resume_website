Privacy Policy

Effective September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bounding Overwatch, developed by Richard Lavey, handles information when you use the iPhone or iPad application.

1. No Account or Developer Collection

Bounding Overwatch does not require or offer an account. The app does not require you to provide a name, email address, phone number, contact list, precise location, or payment information to the developer. You may choose names for local player seats, but the app does not send them, campaign progress, skirmish results, saves, settings, or other gameplay information to Richard Lavey or a developer-operated cloud service.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores information locally so its features work. This may include accessibility, audio, visual, and control settings; campaign progress and medals; names you choose for local player seats; map selections and procedural generation settings; saved match state; and the command history used for match saves and recovery. This information remains in the app's local storage unless you choose nearby LAN play as described below.

3. Player-Initiated Nearby LAN Play

When you choose to host or join a nearby LAN match, Bounding Overwatch uses Apple's MultipeerConnectivity framework to discover and communicate with one nearby compatible device. The session exchanges temporary peer callsigns, app and content compatibility information, lobby readiness, map and rule settings, procedural map parameters when applicable, game commands, match state, results, consistency hashes, and connection or recovery messages needed to play and reconnect.

This traffic is exchanged directly between participating devices and is not routed through a Bounding Overwatch account, internet matchmaking service, analytics service, or developer-operated backend. MultipeerConnectivity sessions require transport encryption. The app does not add a public player directory, text chat, voice chat, contacts access, or precise-location sharing. A connected peer receives the match information needed for the session, so use nearby LAN play only with a player you trust.

4. Local Network Permission

Bounding Overwatch requests local-network access only when it is needed to find or host a nearby LAN match. You can deny or later revoke this permission in iOS or iPadOS Settings. Campaign, AI skirmish, pass-and-play, local settings, and local saves do not require local-network permission.

5. Advertising, Analytics, Tracking, and Purchases

Bounding Overwatch does not include third-party advertising, analytics, cross-app tracking, or an advertising identifier. It does not sell or rent personal information. The app has no subscriptions or in-app purchases and does not collect payment information.

6. Cloud Services and Third Parties

Bounding Overwatch does not use a developer-operated cloud backend for accounts, saves, gameplay, matchmaking, analytics, or advertising. Apple provides the operating-system features used for local storage, local-network permission, and MultipeerConnectivity, and may process technical information under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. This public details and privacy page is hosted by GitHub Pages, which is separate from the app.

7. Retention and Deletion

Local settings, campaign progress, and saved matches remain on your device until they are reset, replaced, deleted within available app controls, or removed with the app. Deleting Bounding Overwatch removes its locally stored data from the device, subject to the normal operation of device backups you control. Nearby LAN discovery ends when you leave the lobby or match. Match information received from a peer may remain in a local match save for the same period as other local gameplay information.

8. Security

Bounding Overwatch relies on Apple platform protections for app storage and requires encrypted MultipeerConnectivity sessions for nearby LAN play. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Bounding Overwatch does not knowingly collect personal information from children through an account or developer-operated service because it has neither. Nearby LAN play exchanges game-session information with the other participating device as described above. The game includes stylized tactical combat.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or privacy practices change. Any revision will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests about Bounding Overwatch, email updates@realexpense.com.
