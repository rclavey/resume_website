Choose your stats

Analyze honestly

Compare win rates, scoring likelihood, and leaderboards alongside 95% confidence intervals and plain-language data warnings.

Data you can use

Record opponents, scores, attendance, player stats, venues, and notes. Export raw league history as CSV or save a polished dashboard summary as a PNG whenever you want to share the results.

PerformIQ reports associations rather than claiming causation. Opponent strength, teammate combinations, substitutions, and other unrecorded context can influence a pattern, and small samples are clearly identified.

Privacy Policy

Last updated July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PerformIQ, developed by Richard Lavey, handles information when you use the iPhone application.

1. Information We Collect

PerformIQ does not require an account and does not collect personal information. The leagues, sports, rosters, player names, game results, statistics, venues, and notes you enter are stored locally on your device.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Information you enter is processed on your device to provide game history, league records, player-impact comparisons, scoring likelihood, statistical leaderboards, confidence intervals, and data-quality guidance.

3. Exports

PerformIQ creates CSV and PNG exports only when you request them. You decide whether and where to save or share an exported file using the standard iOS interface.

4. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

PerformIQ does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies. It does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

App data is stored locally on your device. You can delete individual leagues within the app or erase all PerformIQ data from Settings. Deleting the app also removes its locally stored data, subject to the normal operation of your device and any device backups you control.

6. Children's Privacy

PerformIQ is a general sports statistics app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Any update will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or PerformIQ, email updates@realexpense.com.

Support

For help, feedback, or questions about PerformIQ, email updates@realexpense.com. Please include your iOS version, the sport you are tracking, and a short description of what happened. Do not include sensitive information or private team data.
